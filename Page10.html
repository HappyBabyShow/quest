The Hill of the Cathedral: The Last Judgment Location: Porto Cathedral (Sé do Porto)

👻 The Hill of the Cathedral: The Last Sentence In front of you stands Porto Cathedral, a real stone castle. For hundreds of years, it has watched the life of the city. But behind its great walls, there is a dark story of religious courts, accusations, and punishment. People say that, long ago, people accused of witchcraft, heresy, or working with evil forces were judged in this square. There was a stone post here. The people who were punished were tied to it, so the whole city could see their punishment. According to the legend, one day, a young woman was brought here. People said she could talk to the dead. She said she was innocent. Before her execution, she made a curse: everyone who punishes an innocent person will one day hear their own death sentence from a ghost. Since then, local people say that, on foggy nights, a woman in a long black dress appears near the cathedral. She slowly walks around the place where the old stone post stood. It seems that she is looking for the people who judged her. Sometimes, people hear the sound of chains under the cold stone walls. Then a quiet voice says the same words again and again: «It is your turn…» And if tonight, when you walk past the cathedral, you hear someone say your name and ask you to confess your sins, do not answer. Maybe the trial has already started, and you do not even know what you are accused of…

Spell: Touch the twisted stone column of the old punishment post. Say the magic words to stop the ghosts of the judges from following you.
